The idea

The lab takes the shape of your data

Point lensing at a corpus, declare your target, and the whole lab bends to fit. The brand says this without explaining it: a regular framework grid curves around the mass of your dataset, and an Einstein ring lights where the deflection peaks. It is the textbook picture of mass curving spacetime and a literal picture of a framework conforming to your data, in one image.

The concept card — assets/lattice.svg. Slate so it reads on light and dark alike; the red→orange→blue velocity ramp is reserved for the mark and brand surfaces. Generated, re-runnable: make_lattice.py.

Icon family

The Einstein-ring alignment sweep

Full ring → arcs → single arc is a real physical progression, so each stage of the pipeline carries a different alignment state. Stroke inherits currentColor; --brand-src tints the source dot. They feed the app’s empty-state mark and external surfaces — they do not replace the text-monogram glyphs the app uses for runs, datasets and models.
